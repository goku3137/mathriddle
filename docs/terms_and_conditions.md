## 5. Virtual Energy (Hearts), Points, and Score System

Math Riddle incorporates a cognitive pacing mechanism ("Hearts" or "Energy") and a cumulative scoring system.

### 5.1 Energy Rules and Heart Mechanics
- Players possess a maximum pool of five (5) hearts.
- Submitting an incorrect answer deducts exactly one (1) heart.
- Hearts automatically regenerate at the calibrated rate of one (1) heart every twenty (20) minutes of real-world elapsed time.
- Depleted hearts may alternatively be replenished by watching an optional rewarded video ad or upgrading to PRO status.
- **No Cash Value:** Hearts, score points, tier badges, and leaderboard standings are strictly virtual cognitive tracking metrics. They have no monetary value, cannot be redeemed for fiat currency, merchandise, or legal tender, and cannot be transferred, sold, or gifted to third parties.

### 5.2 Anti-Tamper & Score Integrity Policy
Valcire reserves the right to sanitize, clamp, or reset the total score or level progression of any user if client-side cryptographic verification indicates:
- Device clock manipulation or artificial time-jumping intended to bypass heart refill cooldowns.
- Memory editing or save-file injection resulting in impossible scores exceeding the mathematical maximum (`computeMaximumValidScore`).
- Tampering with cryptographic state signatures (`SecurityViolation.tamperedStorage`).
